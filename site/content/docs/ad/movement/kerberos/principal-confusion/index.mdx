---
title: "Principal Confusion"
description: "Making the KDC issue tickets for the wrong principal"
---

## Theory

Kerberos identifies accounts by attributes that are not always immutable or
unique. If an attacker can change one of them, the KDC can be persuaded to
issue a ticket describing a *different* principal from the one that
authenticated, and the resulting ticket is legitimately signed.

These attacks turn on the relationship between an account's `sAMAccountName`,
its `servicePrincipalName` and the `sname` in a ticket request, and on how the
KDC resolves a principal when those disagree.
